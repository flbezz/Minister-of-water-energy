About the Ministry

Serving the nation through water and energy.

The Ministry of Water & Energy (MoWE) is the federal authority responsible for water resources management, energy development, and the delivery of public services that power Ethiopia's growth.

Our institutional mandate

Our mandate spans river basin governance, rural electrification, renewable energy strategy, scientific research, and citizen-facing digital services.

Direction

What guides the Ministry

Our mission and vision establish the long-term direction of Ethiopia's water and energy development.

01 / MISSION

Our Mission

To drive sustainable national development through equitable water resource management and universal access to clean, affordable, renewable energy — delivered with transparency, innovation, and public accountability.

02 / VISION

Our Vision

A Ethiopia where every citizen has reliable access to safe water and clean energy, supported by world-class infrastructure, scientific research, and digital public services.

Accountability

An accountable institution

The Ministry is committed to responsible governance, transparent decision-making, and measurable public service. Accountability is embedded in how we plan, manage resources, deliver services, and report results.

01

Transparent governance

Making institutional information, decisions, and public resources more accessible to citizens.

02

Responsible resource management

Managing national water and energy resources with efficiency, sustainability, and long-term public value in mind.

03

Performance and reporting

Measuring institutional performance and communicating progress toward national development objectives.

04

Citizen-centered services

Improving access to reliable information, digital services, and public-facing programs.
