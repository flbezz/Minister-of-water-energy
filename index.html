Message from the Minister
"Water and energy are the foundations upon which a modern, prosperous Ethiopia is built."
Our Ministry is committed to expanding access to clean water and renewable electricity in every corner of the country. Through investment in hydropower, solar, wind, and modern water systems, we are laying the groundwork for sustainable development that reaches every Ethiopian citizen.
